From one factor to a usable private key
  1. Complete the factorizationq = N / pVerify p × q = N and distinct prime factors.
  2. Compute the group exponentλ(N) = lcm(p − 1, q − 1)Require gcd(e, λ(N)) = 1.
  3. Recover the private exponentd = e−1 mod λ(N)Check key consistency before OAEP decryption.
A recovered private key permits ordinary OAEP decryption.
The weakness lies in shared key factors. OAEP itself is not broken by this experiment.
